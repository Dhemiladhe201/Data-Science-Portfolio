Customer behaviour

Revenue is calculated from completed orders. Customers enter a cohort in their first completed-purchase month, not when they sign up.

Refunds are excluded rather than netted off, and acquisition channels have no associated spend data. Channel revenue therefore does not represent advertising return on investment.

The segmentation thresholds and all displayed results come from a simulated dataset, not live customer observations.
